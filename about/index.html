About Alhambra Pro Plumbing

A dispatch line, licensed plumbers and a clear price before work starts.

☎ Call (626) 898-6169

Who we are

Alhambra Pro Plumbing is a call-dispatched plumbing service based at 49 S Garfield Ave in Alhambra, California. Our job is to answer the phone at any hour, work out what is wrong and send a licensed plumber to fix it. The address is a dispatch address, so please call before you drive over.

We cover Alhambra and the neighboring towns of South Pasadena, San Marino, Monterey Park, San Gabriel and Rosemead. See the areas page for details on each.

How we work

You call, describe the problem and get shutoff advice right away. A plumber is dispatched and arrives with a diagnosis and a clear price before any work begins. If the job changes once a wall or a pipe is open, we stop and tell you before going further.

Our plumbers are licensed and insured for the work they do. We do not publish license numbers on this site, and we do not post ratings or reviews we cannot show. If you want to confirm a license, ask the plumber who arrives and check it with the Contractors State License Board.

What we do

Emergency work comes first: burst pipes, clogged drains, water heater failures and sewer backups. Planned work follows, including water heater replacement, repiping and plumbing installation. See the full list of services.

What happens when you call

A dispatcher answers and asks four things: your address, what you see, whether water is still running and whether anyone is in danger. If water is flowing, you get shutoff steps first. If gas is involved, you are told to leave the building and call the gas utility from outside.

Next, a plumber is assigned and you get a realistic arrival window. The plumber calls ahead, arrives, finds the cause and tells you what the fix involves and what it costs. You decide, and work begins only when you say yes.

What we will not do

We will not quote a number over the phone for a problem nobody has seen and then change it at your door. We will not push a replacement when a repair will hold. We will not pad a visit with work you did not ask for.

We will also tell you when a job belongs to someone else. A leak on the street side of your water meter is a matter for your water utility. A grease interceptor needs a licensed hauler. A permit question goes to the city, and we point you to the right office.

Checking a plumber

You can verify any California contractor license on the Contractors State License Board website. Ask for the plumber's name and license number before work begins, and check that it is active and in the right classification. A reputable plumber expects the question.

What we publish and what we do not

This site lists the services we offer, the towns we cover and the local facts we could verify from public sources, such as the City of Alhambra water quality report and municipal code. We do not show star ratings, testimonials or counts of years or jobs, because we cannot prove them on a page. When we cite a fact, the source type is named.

If something on this site looks wrong, please tell us by email or by phone, and we will check it and fix it.

Anyone calling a plumber in an emergency is stressed and unsure what they are looking at. Our approach is to slow the conversation down, explain what we see in plain words and let you decide at each step.

Our promise on pricing

A clear price before work starts means a number you can accept or decline. It covers the labor and parts we describe. If we find something new, we stop, explain it and let you choose. Night, weekend and holiday visits cost more than weekday ones, and you hear that before you agree.

Where we answer calls

Our service area follows the roads. Garfield Avenue and Fremont Avenue lead north to San Marino and South Pasadena, Atlantic Boulevard and Garfield Avenue lead south into Monterey Park, and Valley Boulevard and Mission Road lead east to San Gabriel and Rosemead. Dispatch is organized around those routes so a plumber is rarely far from you.

Why local knowledge matters

Alhambra has 26 historic single-family neighborhoods, many built in the 1930s, and water with hardness above 200 ppm according to the City's 2024 water quality report. Those two facts shape the work. Old pipe fails in predictable ways, and hard water leaves scale in heaters and valves. We plan for both.

Call recording and your privacy

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Read the privacy policy and the terms of service for details.
