How the area pages differ

Each town page covers different ground. South Pasadena looks at the City's own wells and its mix of renters and owners. San Marino covers large lots, 1920 to 1950 homes and the utility's published hardness. Monterey Park covers hillside pressure, shopping centers and the City water division. San Gabriel covers seasons, shared building drains and food businesses. Rosemead covers dense lots and sewer laterals along Garvey Avenue.

Read the page for your town before you call, and have your street and the nearest cross street ready for the dispatcher.
