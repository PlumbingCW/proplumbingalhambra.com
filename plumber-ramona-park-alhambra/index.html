Plumber in Ramona Park, Alhambra, CA

Written by the Alhambra Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 14, 2026

Ramona Park is a southern Alhambra neighborhood of early twentieth century Arts and Crafts homes. This page explains what a sewer camera inspection can tell an owner and when it is worth doing.

Where Ramona Park sits

Ramona Park Tract is in southern Alhambra, according to the Discover Alhambra History neighborhood guide. Real estate directories also list East Ramona Park and South Ramona, and this page treats them as part of one area instead of splitting them. Boundaries are not documented in the sources we found.

Homes in Ramona Park

Local sources describe early 1900s Arts and Crafts homes designed by the architects John and Daniel Althouse. These are homes with long histories, and many have been remodeled since.

Why a camera inspection helps

A drain that backs up more than once is usually telling you something about the main line. A sewer camera shows what is inside the pipe, including breaks, offsets, roots and buildup. It replaces guessing with a picture and a location.
