Water Softener and Filtration in Alhambra, CA

Written by the Alhambra Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 14, 2026

Water softener and filtration service in Alhambra addresses a local fact: the City of Alhambra's 2024 water quality report shows hardness of 202 ppm in its groundwater and 272 ppm in imported water, as calcium carbonate. That is very hard by the usual scale, and it explains the scale on fixtures, in water heaters and on glass.

What the City of Alhambra's report actually says

The City's 2024 annual water quality report states that about 70 percent of the supply is local groundwater from the Main San Gabriel Basin, and the rest comes through a connection with the Metropolitan Water District. That water is treated surface water from the Colorado River and the State Water Project.

Total hardness as calcium carbonate is 202 ppm for groundwater, with a range of 110 to 314, and 272 ppm for the imported water, with a range of 241 to 303.

Divide ppm by about 17 to get grains per gallon. That puts the groundwater near 12 gpg and the imported water near 16 gpg. The US Geological Survey's usual scale calls anything above 180 mg/L very hard. Your tap may be a mix, depending on where you live in the city and the time of year.

Softener versus filter: they do different jobs

A softener removes calcium and magnesium by ion exchange, which prevents scale. A filter removes sediment, chlorine taste or specific contaminants depending on the media. A reverse osmosis unit at one tap cleans drinking water. None of them do the others' job.

The City's report is clear that the water meets standards, and softening is a comfort and equipment-protection choice. It mentions softening as one way to reduce calcium carbonate buildup on fixtures and in the hot water system. We do not claim a system makes water safer unless that is true for the product.

Safety checklist: what to do around water treatment equipment

  • ✓Keep salt bags dry and off the floor, and store them away from children and pets.
  • ✓Never mix cleaners or chemicals into a softener brine tank.
  • ✓Close the bypass valve before servicing any unit, then reopen it afterward.
  • ✓Know where the unit drain discharges and keep it from freezing or flooding.
  • ✓If you are on a low-sodium diet, ask your doctor before drinking softened water.

Honest limits

Softened water adds sodium in small amounts, and some people on sodium-restricted diets prefer a separate drinking tap. Salt-free scale reducers change how scale forms but do not remove hardness. We explain which type you are buying. We make no health claims, and for any health question the right person is your physician.

If you are choosing a heater, hardness is worth considering at purchase. See water heater installation and tankless water heater for how scale affects each type. A softener install also touches piping, which is part of plumbing installation.

Existing softeners and conditioners also need care. If yours is not softening, check the salt level, the bypass valve and the control settings before calling. A service visit tests incoming and outgoing hardness, inspects the resin tank and the brine line and tells you honestly whether a repair or a replacement makes sense.
