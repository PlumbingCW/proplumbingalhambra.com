Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Alhambra, CA

Written by the Alhambra Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 14, 2026

Slab leak repair in Alhambra fixes a water line that has started leaking under or inside a concrete foundation. It shows up as warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water with every tap off, damp flooring or a water meter that spins by itself, and it needs a plumber with detection tools, not guesswork.

When a slab leak is an emergency

A slab leak can run for weeks before it shows, but flooring that is lifting, water coming through baseboards or a wet slab that is steaming is urgent. The 24/7 line is open. In the meantime:

What to do while you wait

  1. Turn the main water valve to the house off, clockwise, to stop the leak feeding.
  2. Turn off the water heater to avoid damage if the hot line is the leak source.
  3. Move rugs and furniture off wet flooring and open windows to move air.
  4. Note meter readings and take photos of where floors feel warm or damp.

Slab leaks in context: which Alhambra homes have slabs

Homes of the 1930s, such as those in the Mayfair Tract and much of the Bean Tract, were typically built on raised foundations with a crawlspace, though not all of them. Slab-on-grade construction became more common after World War II. Postwar Minimal Traditional homes, including those in the Airport Tract on the old Alhambra Airport site, are the kind of housing where a slab is more likely.

That is a general pattern and not a promise about your address. The foundation type decides whether a leaking line sits in a crawlspace you can inspect or inside concrete you cannot, so the first question is what is under the house.

Repair choices

Once the leak is pinpointed, there are three typical paths. A spot repair breaks the slab at the leak and replaces the damaged section. A reroute abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through walls or the attic. A repipe replaces the supply system when several leaks suggest the pipe is failing along its length.

The right path depends on how many leaks there are, the age of the pipe and the layout of the house. We explain each one with its cost in plain words so you can choose. If the evidence points to several failing lines, see repiping.

Questions to ask before a slab repair

  • ✓Exactly where is the leak, and how was it confirmed?
  • ✓Is a spot repair, a reroute or a repipe the better fit for this house?
  • ✓How will the floor and finishes be restored, and who is responsible for that?
  • ✓Is a permit needed? Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Alhambra at (626) 570-5007.
  • ✓What does the warranty cover?

Insurance and slab leaks

Insurance coverage for slab leaks varies widely. Some policies cover resulting damage but not the cost of repairing the pipe itself, and some cover the access cost. Ask your carrier before work begins and keep the plumber's written findings, photos and invoice. We do not give coverage opinions.

Timing
An active leak at night is priced above a scheduled day visit.
