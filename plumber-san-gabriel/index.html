What changes with the seasons

Southern California plumbing problems follow the weather. In the dry months, sewer roots grow toward the moisture in joints and cracks. When the first heavy rain arrives, debris and ground movement test those same weak points, and backups show up. A camera pass before the wet season costs less than a sewer overflow in the front yard.

In the cooler months, water heaters work harder and old tanks fail. A rusty drip at the base, a rumbling noise or lukewarm water are signs to book a water heater repair visit before the tank gives out. A tank that is more than ten years old is worth a close look.

What to do while you wait

  1. Turn off the main water valve. In apartments, look for the unit shutoff under the sink or behind the toilet.
  2. Switch off the breaker for wet areas.
  3. Lift belongings off the floor and put towels at doorways to slow the water.
  4. Call our 24/7 line and say whether the leak is in a unit, a shared wall or the yard.

Apartments, condos and shared pipes

More than half of San Gabriel's occupied homes are rented, per the 2020 census figures. Many sit in multi-unit buildings where drains and risers are shared. A backup in one unit may start in a line serving six. We trace where the blockage sits, document it and give the written findings the manager or owner needs.

If you rent, shut off water to your unit and report the problem right away. Most owners will approve an emergency call. A running toilet or a drip may look small, but see toilet repair and faucet repair because they waste water and raise the bill.

Kitchens and food businesses near San Gabriel Square

San Gabriel Square is a 12-acre mall, and the streets around it are full of restaurants, bakeries and tea shops. Kitchens produce grease, and grease builds into a hard plug that cabling alone does not clear. Hydro jetting cuts it back to clean pipe. A regular schedule beats an emergency at dinner service.

Grease rules apply to food businesses. The City of Alhambra's Environmental Compliance Division handles grease in Alhambra, and San Gabriel has its own city offices, so ask the City of San Gabriel what your kitchen needs. Our commercial plumbing team takes care of the drain side.
