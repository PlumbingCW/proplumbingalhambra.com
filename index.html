Alhambra knowledge

What is different about plumbing in Alhambra

Older neighborhoods

Alhambra is promoted as a city of homes, and Wikipedia reports 26 historic single-family neighborhoods, including the Bean, Midwick, Airport and Mayfair tracts. Many date from the early to mid twentieth century, and a plumber can tell you what your own house has.

Very hard water

According to the City of Alhambra's 2024 water quality report, hardness is 202 ppm in local groundwater and 272 ppm in imported water. That means scale in water heaters, on fixtures and inside valves.

Busy commercial streets

Valley Boulevard, Main Street and Garfield Avenue carry restaurants, shops and offices. Grease lines, floor drains and after-hours repairs are part of the work.

Local code

The City adopted the Los Angeles County plumbing code in Chapter 20.11 of its municipal code. Ask the City at (626) 570-5007 what a permit covers for your job.
