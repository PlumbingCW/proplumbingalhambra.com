Where Bean Tract sits

Bean Tract is in the northeastern tip of Alhambra, according to the Discover Alhambra History neighborhood guide. The land was once owned by the orchardist Jacob Bean, a fact the city's Wikipedia entry also notes. Most homes date from the 1930s and 1940s.

Homes in Bean Tract

Local sources describe Colonial Revival and Tudor Revival houses, and the preservation group lists Minimal Traditional homes here too. After eighty years, most houses have had bathrooms and kitchens updated at least once.
