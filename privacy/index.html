Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

☎ Call (626) 898-6169

Effective November 14, 2026. This policy explains what information Alhambra Pro Plumbing collects through this website and by phone.

What we collect

This website has no forms and no accounts, so it does not collect personal information you type. When you call, we hear your voice, your phone number and whatever details you give us about the problem and the address.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How we use it

We use call details to dispatch a plumber, give you a price, follow up on work and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map. Those services may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. Their own policies apply.

Cookies and analytics

We do not set advertising cookies on this site.

Your choices

You may ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it, subject to legal and record-keeping duties. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. Contact us at contact@proplumbingalhambra.com or by phone.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new effective date. See also the terms of service.
