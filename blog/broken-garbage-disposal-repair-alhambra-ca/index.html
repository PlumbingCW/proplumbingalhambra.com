Broken Garbage Disposal in Alhambra, CA

Written by the Alhambra Pro Plumbing team · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

A garbage disposal that hums but does not spin is usually jammed. Turn off the switch, then use the hex socket on the underside of the unit with an Allen wrench to rotate the motor back and forth until it moves freely. Press the red reset button on the bottom and try again. If it is silent, leaking from the body or smells of burning, call a plumber, because a leaking disposal cannot be repaired.

How to clear a jam safely

Free a jammed disposal

  1. Turn off the wall switch and unplug the unit or turn off its breaker. Never put a hand in the chamber.
  2. Look into the opening with a flashlight and use tongs to remove anything you see, such as a bottle cap or utensil.
  3. Insert a hex wrench into the socket at the center of the underside and turn it back and forth until the flywheel moves freely.
  4. Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit.
  5. Restore power, run cold water and test the switch.

If it jams again right away, there may be damage inside or the motor may be failing. Stop using it, and book a repair visit.

What not to put down a disposal

  • ✓Grease and oil, which harden and clog the line.
  • ✓Bones, fruit pits, glass, metal or plastic.
  • ✓Large amounts of coffee grounds, eggshells or pasta.
  • ✓Fibrous vegetables such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.
  • ✓Chemical drain cleaners, which can damage the seals and splash on you.

Repair or replace

A jam or a reset is a repair. A cracked housing, a motor that will not turn or a leak from the body means replacement. Most disposals last eight to fifteen years, and a unit near the end of that range with a leak is not worth fixing.

When a new unit goes in, the plumber reconnects the dishwasher drain, checks the knockout plug on the inlet and tests the electrical connection. If the sink drain is slow even after the disposal runs, the problem is farther down. A drain cleaning visit will find it.

Emergency or not?

If the disposal leaks into the cabinet, call the same day and put a bucket under it. If it is jammed but dry, you can wait for a normal appointment. If the unit smells of burning, turn it off at the breaker. See garbage disposal repair for our process.

Choosing a replacement disposal

Disposals are sold by motor power, usually from one third to one horsepower. A larger motor grinds faster and jams less, and it costs more. Continuous feed units run while you push scraps in. Batch feed units run only when the stopper is in place, which some owners prefer for safety.

Look at sound insulation, the mount type and the warranty length. Check that the new unit fits under the sink with room for the trap and the dishwasher hose, because some models are taller than the one they replace.

Everyday care that extends disposal life

  • ✓Run cold water for fifteen seconds after use so grease moves down the line.
  • ✓Grind a few ice cubes now and then to scrape the walls, if the maker allows it.
  • ✓Never run the unit dry, and never use it as a trash can.
  • ✓Rinse the flange with warm soapy water to stop odors.
  • ✓Replace the unit if the reset button trips every time.

What a plumber checks on a repair visit

A plumber starts with the switch and the circuit, because a dead outlet looks like a dead motor. Next comes the reset button and the flywheel. If the unit turns freely, the plumber checks the mounting ring, the gasket and the drain connections for the source of any leak.

Then the discharge side gets a look. A disposal that runs fine but leaves the sink full of water points to a clog in the trap arm or the wall stub. That is a drain job and not a disposal job, and a good plumber says so instead of selling a new unit.

Safety checklist: what to do before you touch a disposal

  • ✓Turn off the wall switch and the breaker, or unplug the unit.
  • ✓Never put fingers or hands in the chamber, even with the power off.
  • ✓Use tongs, pliers or a wooden spoon handle to remove objects.
  • ✓Wear gloves and eye protection if a sink is backed up with waste.
  • ✓Call a plumber if the unit smells of burning or sparks.

Alhambra water and kitchen drains

According to the City of Alhambra's 2024 water quality report, groundwater hardness is 202 ppm and imported water hardness is 272 ppm. Hard water leaves scale on seals and inside the drain connection, which makes leaks more likely as a disposal ages.

The City's Environmental Compliance Division covers wastewater, stormwater and drinking water compliance. For questions about what can go down the public sewer, contact the City of Alhambra Utilities Department at (626) 570-5061. For food businesses, ask the City what grease rules apply.
