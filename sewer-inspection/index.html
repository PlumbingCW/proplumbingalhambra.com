Sewer Inspection in Alhambra, CA

Written by the Alhambra Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 14, 2026

A sewer inspection in Alhambra sends a waterproof camera through your drain or sewer line and records exactly what is in the pipe. It turns a guess about cracks, roots or sags into video you can see, which is why it is worth doing before a repair or a home purchase.

☎ Call (626) 898-6169All services

What a sewer camera inspection shows

A technician feeds a flexible cable with a camera head through the cleanout. The head has a light and a locator, so the plumber can mark on the surface where a defect sits and how deep it is. The video is recorded for you.

The inspection reveals roots entering at joints, cracked or broken pipe, offset joints, scale and grease buildup, a sag that holds water, and lateral connections that are not where they should be. It also shows the pipe material, which tells you what to expect from the rest of the line.

When an inspection is worth it

Good reasons to book one

  • ✓A drain has been cleared more than once in a year and keeps backing up.
  • ✓You are buying a house and want to know what sits under the lawn before closing.
  • ✓A plumber's cable stalled at a fixed distance and could not get through.
  • ✓You can smell sewage outdoors or see a damp, sunken patch along the pipe path.
  • ✓You are planning a remodel that adds a bathroom to the existing line.

For a buyer, the camera is a cheap way to see a lateral that may be 80 years old. Alhambra's older tracts include the Mayfair Tract, built in the 1930s, the Bean Tract, with many homes from the 1930s and 1940s, and the Midwick Tract from the 1940s. A house of that age could have its original pipe in places, and sellers rarely know the condition.

After the inspection

You get the recorded video and a plain explanation of what was seen, with distances. If the line is clear, you can stop worrying. If it is damaged, the plumber lays out the repair choices on our sewer line repair page, and a line that only needs cleaning moves to sewer line cleaning or hydro jetting.

An inspection does not replace a licensed home inspector's report on the house, and we do not give legal or insurance opinions. What it gives you is evidence about the pipe.

What shapes the price

Inspection pricing depends on these points:

Access
A usable cleanout makes the job quicker. Pulling a toilet to enter the line takes longer.
Length of line
Longer runs take more time to scan and record.
Cleaning first
A line packed with grease or paper needs cleaning before the camera can see it.
Locating
Marking the depth and position of a defect on the surface adds time.
Report
A recorded video and written findings are part of the job so you can share them with a buyer, a seller or a repair contractor.

You get a clear price before work starts. This is a planned service in most cases, so it is not billed as an emergency unless an active backup is what prompted the call.

Where this page draws its facts

Drain and sewer work follows the UPC-based California Plumbing Code, which sets rules for traps, vents, cleanouts and slope. Alhambra adopted the Los Angeles County plumbing code in Chapter 20.11 of its municipal code, and the Utilities Department at (626) 570-5061 can tell you who maintains the public sewer on your street.

Cost and process notes come from how plumbers actually price and perform this work, not from a published price list, so ask for the price before work starts.
