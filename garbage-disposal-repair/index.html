Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Alhambra, CA

Written by the Alhambra Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 14, 2026

Garbage disposal repair in Alhambra covers a unit that hums but will not spin, leaks under the sink, smells or has stopped working entirely. The same visit includes a new installation when the old unit is worn out, along with the sink drain connection and electrical hookup.

When a disposal problem needs fast help

A disposal that leaks into the cabinet or backs the sink up with food waste can ruin a cabinet floor and cause odor quickly. Call our 24/7 line. In the meantime:

What to do while you wait

  1. Turn the disposal switch off. Never put a hand into the unit, even when it is off.
  2. If it is leaking, place a bucket under it and clear the cabinet.
  3. Turn off the power at the breaker if the unit is wet or sparking and you can reach the panel safely.
  4. Stop running water in the sink to avoid adding to the overflow.

What causes disposals to jam, leak or stop

Disposal symptoms and responses
SymptomWhat it usually isWhat we do

No sound at allTripped reset button or a dead motorTest the reset and power supply, replace if burned out

A leak from the body of the unit cannot be repaired. Once the housing leaks, the answer is a new disposal. A jam can often be cleared on the spot and the unit kept in service. Plumbers can tell the difference within a few minutes of opening the cabinet.

Alhambra kitchens and disposal wear

Kitchens in older Alhambra homes were laid out before disposals were common, and many were added later. In houses from the 1930s and 1940s, the sink drain and trap may be older than the unit hooked to it, and mineral scale from very hard water narrows the pipe.

The City of Alhambra's 2024 water quality report lists hardness at 202 ppm in groundwater and 272 ppm in imported water. Scale on a disposal's seals and drain connections makes leaks more likely as the unit ages. If the disposal is over ten years old and leaking, replacement is usually the better value.

What to feed and not feed a disposal

  • ✓Run cold water before, during and after use for about 15 seconds.
  • ✓Avoid grease, coffee grounds in volume, pasta, rice and fibrous vegetables like celery and corn husks.
  • ✓Never put bones, glass, metal or plastic in the unit.
  • ✓Cut large scraps into small pieces and feed them slowly.
  • ✓Do not use chemical drain cleaners in a sink with a disposal.

Installing a new disposal

Choose a horsepower to match use. A larger motor handles more load but costs more. The plumber also reconnects the dishwasher drain, checks the knockout plug and tests the electrical hookup, whether the unit is hardwired or plugged into an outlet under the sink.

If the drain line from the sink is slow or fails to clear after a new disposal goes in, the blockage is further down, and a drain cleaning visit will find it. If there is any sign of water in the cabinet beyond the disposal, the supply lines may be the problem and faucet and fixture repair covers that.
