Plumber in Lindaraxa, Alhambra, CA

Lindaraxa is a northern Alhambra neighborhood of 1920s homes. This page covers water heaters, which are the plumbing item most homeowners replace at some point.

Where Lindaraxa sits

Lindaraxa Tract is in northern Alhambra, according to the Discover Alhambra History neighborhood guide. The guide dates it to the 1920s. Some directories call it Lindaraxa Park. If you are unsure whether your street counts, give the dispatcher your address and we will cover it either way.

Homes in Lindaraxa

Local sources describe Spanish, English Cottage and Storybook styles, and a Mission Revival home. Houses from the 1920s have often been updated, and a water heater is one of the parts most often replaced.

Water heater repair or replacement

A leak from the tank body means replacement. A leak at a fitting, a bad thermostat or a pilot that will not stay lit can often be repaired. Our water heater repair page lists the common faults.

Signs it may be time to replace the heater

  • ✓Water pools under the tank and the leak is from the tank body, not a pipe fitting.
  • ✓Rust-colored hot water, rumbling or popping from sediment in the tank.
  • ✓Repairs have become frequent and the heater is past its expected life.
  • ✓The heater cannot keep up with the household any more.
  • ✓You are planning a remodel and want a different type or location.

California requires strapping

California Health and Safety Code Section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced or strapped against earthquake movement. A new installation should include it. Ask the installer to show you how the heater is secured.

If you are choosing between types, see tankless water heaters and water heater installation. For the rest of the city, see [[|plumbing in Alhambra]]. We also serve San Marino to the north.

About these Lindaraxa facts

The northern location, the 1920s era and the home styles come from a local history guide. The strapping rule is a statewide law. We do not claim that Lindaraxa homes have older or failing water heaters than other neighborhoods.
