Emergency Water Heater Repair in Alhambra, CA

Written by the Alhambra Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 14, 2026

Water heater repair in Alhambra covers the faults that leave a home without hot water or put water on the floor: failed thermostats and elements, pilot and burner problems, worn anode rods, leaking valves and a tank that has started to rust through. A licensed plumber is dispatched 24/7, and the first job is to make the situation safe.

When a water heater problem is an emergency

Water pooling around the base of a tank, a smell of gas near a gas heater or a tank that is hot and rumbling all need quick action. Call our 24/7 line. While you wait:

What to do while you wait

  1. For a leak, shut the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the heater by turning it clockwise.
  2. For a smell of gas, leave the house, do not touch switches, and call the gas utility or 911 first. Then call us.
  3. For an electric heater leak, turn off its breaker if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  4. Move anything stored near the heater and keep children away from hot surfaces and standing water.

A leak from a fitting, a relief valve or a drain valve can often be fixed. A leak from the tank itself cannot. Once a tank wall is breached, the answer is water heater replacement, and we will tell you so rather than sell a repair that will not hold.

Hard water is the quiet cause of many failures

Alhambra's water is very hard. The City of Alhambra's 2024 water quality report gives 202 ppm hardness for its groundwater and 272 ppm for imported Metropolitan water, as calcium carbonate. In grains per gallon that is about 12 and 16. Scale builds on the bottom of a tank, covers heating elements and shortens the life of a tank that is never flushed.

Draining a few gallons from the tank each year and checking the anode rod are cheap steps that slow scale and corrosion. If your tank has not been looked at in years, a service visit can find out whether it is worth repairing.

Repair or replace, a quick test

  • ✓How old is the tank? Check the date code on the label. Many tank units are near the end of their life after about a decade, depending on water and care.
  • ✓Is the leak at a fitting or from the tank body? A fitting is repairable.
  • ✓Has the heater needed more than one repair in the last year?
  • ✓Is the tank rusty at the bottom or showing mineral crust around fittings?
  • ✓Does a repair cost a large share of a new unit once labor is counted?

Safety items we check on every heater

California requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake movement. The state's Health and Safety Code section 19211 covers new, replacement and existing residential water heaters up to 120 gallons. If your heater is not strapped, the plumber can fix that during the visit.

We also check the temperature and pressure relief valve and its discharge pipe, venting and combustion air on gas units, and the shutoff valve. These are small items that decide whether a water heater is safe, not only whether it heats.

Gas water heaters: smell of gas and pilot problems

If you smell gas near a heater, do not light anything, do not flip switches and leave the house. Call the gas utility's emergency line from outside, then call us for the repair after the utility clears the home. A heater that will not stay lit may have a dirty pilot assembly, a failing thermocouple or a venting problem, and the venting cause is a carbon monoxide risk that should not wait.

Gas line work follows the California Plumbing Code and needs a permit in most cases, so ask the City at (626) 570-5007 what applies to your job. We do not guess at gas connections, and we test every joint before we leave.

Where a water heater leaks and what to do

A tank that leaks from the body cannot be repaired, and the answer is replacement. Leaks at the fittings, the relief valve discharge, the drain valve or the supply lines are repairable and are far more common than a failed tank. The plumber dries the area and watches where the first drop appears.

If the tank is leaking steadily, shut the cold water supply at the valve above it, turn off the gas or power and drain the tank to the floor drain or outside. No hot water at all has a different set of suspects, namely the thermostat, the heating elements on electric units and the pilot or gas valve on gas units.

We repair water heaters in 91801, 91803 and 91804, and answer calls from 91802 addresses too.
